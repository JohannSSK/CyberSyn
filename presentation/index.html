CyberSyn · AI Security Gateway04 / 06
04 — why it holds up

Four rules we refused to break.

What makes a gateway trustworthy rather than just another blocker in the way.

01

Local first

Scanning runs on the machine serving CyberSyn. No third-party DLP sees the prompt — that would only move the leak.

02

Fail closed

A scanner that crashes, times out or prints nothing parseable refuses the request. A broken gate is never an open gate.

03

Explain, don’t just block

Every block names the stage, category, line, offsets and snippet, so people learn the rule instead of fighting it.

04

Humans stay in control

An administrator can approve a real exception — verified server-side and recorded in the audit trail, attributed.
